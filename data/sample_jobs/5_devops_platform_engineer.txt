Title: DevOps Engineer, Cloudmere

About the role
Cloudmere runs the infrastructure for several SaaS products. You will make deployments boring and reliable.

Responsibilities
- Manage infrastructure on AWS using Terraform and Infrastructure as Code practices.
- Operate Kubernetes clusters and package services with Docker and Helm.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
- Improve observability with Prometheus and Grafana, and respond to incidents.

Requirements
- 3+ years in DevOps, SRE, or platform engineering.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform.
- Strong Linux and Bash skills; scripting in Python.
- Experience with AWS or another cloud provider.

Nice to have
- Ansible
- ArgoCD
- Experience with Datadog or OpenTelemetry
