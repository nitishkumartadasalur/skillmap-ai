Title: Machine Learning Engineer, Kestrel AI

About the role
Kestrel AI builds language models for customer support teams. You will take models from research to production and keep them healthy once they are live.

Responsibilities
- Train, evaluate, and fine-tune NLP models with PyTorch and Hugging Face.
- Build and deploy model-serving APIs in Python and Docker.
- Own MLOps workflows: experiment tracking with MLflow, automated training pipelines, and monitoring.
- Collaborate with product and data teams on feature engineering and evaluation.

Requirements
- 3+ years of machine learning experience with Python.
- Solid knowledge of deep learning, NLP, and scikit-learn.
- Experience deploying models on AWS or another cloud platform.
- Familiarity with SQL, Git, and CI/CD.

Nice to have
- Kubernetes
- Apache Spark
- Experience with LLMs, RAG, or LangChain
- TensorFlow
