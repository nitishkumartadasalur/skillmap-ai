Title: Data Analyst, Brightwave Analytics

About the role
Brightwave helps subscription businesses understand customer behaviour. As a data analyst you will turn product and revenue data into clear answers for our clients.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL to query and analyse large datasets.
- Build dashboards in Power BI or Tableau and present insights to stakeholders.
- Design and evaluate A/B tests and report results with sound statistical analysis.
- Clean and prepare data with Python (pandas) and Excel.

Requirements
- 2+ years in an analytics role.
- Advanced SQL and strong data visualization skills.
- Working knowledge of Python and statistics, including hypothesis testing.
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI.
- Clear communication with non-technical audiences.

Nice to have
- dbt and Snowflake
- Airflow
- Experience with Google Analytics
